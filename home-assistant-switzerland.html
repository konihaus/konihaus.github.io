What is Home Assistant?

One hub for all your devices.

Home Assistant is a free, open-source smart home platform. It brings lights, sensors, heating, blinds, cameras and plugs from different manufacturers onto one shared interface — instead of one app per device.

Data is processed locally, and automations keep running even without internet. This is the foundation I use for Greenhub, the heart of your home.

The platform is powerful, but not everyone wants to install, update and secure it themselves. I take care of that: from the first consultation to ongoing remote management.
