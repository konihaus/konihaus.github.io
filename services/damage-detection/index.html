Security & Protection

Damage detection

Water, smoke and CO detectors that do more than beep: they trigger automations — lights on, a push notification to your contacts, the water valve closed where one exists. So damage is noticed sooner, not the next morning.

Free consultation

What's included

Water leak sensors

Under the washing machine, dishwasher, water heater or in the basement — they report even a few drops.

Smoke and CO detectors

Connected: when one goes off, everyone knows — including whoever is not at home.

Automatic reaction

Lights on, blinds up, heating off — and, where present, a water valve that closes.

Frost and humidity

Notices when there is frost risk or humidity stays high, before mould forms.

What it looks like day to day

  • At 2 a.m. the washing machine hose bursts — your phone reports it right away instead of in the morning.
  • The smoke detector in the basement goes off and the lights come on throughout the house.
  • In the empty holiday flat the temperature drops towards zero — you get a notice in time.

Technology & devices

The detectors are placed or stuck on and run on Zigbee — no changes to the flat, so they work for renters too. Notifications arrive as push messages via the free app that is part of the offer. An automatic water valve I only use where a specialist firm can install it on the main line.

Konihaus is not an emergency service: notifications go to the contacts you choose. The technology raises the chance that something is noticed early — it is not a guarantee.

Cost

This service is part of the Konihaus packages — from CHF 39 per month, with installation, setup and ongoing support included. How much your home needs we work out in the free consultation. You then get a quote that lists every item separately. See packages & prices
