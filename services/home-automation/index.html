Smart Home & Automation

Home automation & retrofitting

Lights, blinds, heating and appliances work together instead of each living in its own app. I connect what you already have, add what is missing and retrofit classic switches and blinds — locally, with no ecosystem lock-in.

Free consultation With specialist partner

What's included

One hub for everything

Greenhub, based on Home Assistant, connects devices from different brands — Zigbee, Matter, Thread and Wi-Fi.

Your existing devices

Whatever is already smart, I integrate. New hardware is added only where it really makes a difference.

Retrofit instead of replace

Flush-mounted actuators, relays and smart switches make existing lamps, blinds and appliances controllable.

Automations that think ahead

Routines based on time of day, presence or weather — so you don't have to operate anything day to day.

What it looks like day to day

  • When you leave the house in the evening, lights and appliances switch off and the blinds come down.
  • The existing switch in the hallway stays — it now also controls a whole lighting scene.
  • Instead of five apps you open one — or none, because most of it runs by itself.

Technology & devices

At the heart is Greenhub, the local hub based on Home Assistant. Automations run in your home, even without internet. For retrofitting I use proven actuators that fit behind the existing switch or into the flush-mounted box; work on the fixed electrical installation is done by a licensed electrician.

One point of contact — even when specialists are needed.

Some work belongs in the hands of specialists: connections to the fixed electrical installation, new cabling or construction work. For this I bring in qualified firms, such as licensed electricians. You still get one quote, I coordinate everything, and you keep talking only to me.

Cost

This service is part of the Konihaus packages — from CHF 39 per month, with installation, setup and ongoing support included. How much your home needs we work out in the free consultation. You then get a quote that lists every item separately. See packages & prices
