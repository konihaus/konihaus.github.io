Smart Home & Automation

Blinds & shading

Blinds and shutters move with sun, wind, temperature or absence. In summer the house stays cool, in a storm the slats are protected, and while you are away the house looks lived in.

Free consultation

What's included

Sun protection by sun position

The blinds shade the side of the house the sun is shining on — not simply all of them at the same time.

Wind and storm protection

A wind sensor raises the slats in time, before they get damaged.

Temperature and heating

Against overheating in summer, letting in solar warmth in winter — coordinated with the heating.

Holiday and away mode

Blinds move at varying times so the house looks lived in.

What it looks like day to day

  • On a hot afternoon only the blinds on the south-west side close — the other rooms stay bright.
  • When a thunderstorm comes in, all slats go up, even if nobody is home.
  • In the morning the bedroom blinds open slowly together with the alarm.

Technology & devices

Existing electric blinds and shutters I integrate through their current control or a flush-mounted actuator, depending on the motor. Wind, sun and temperature values come from a sensor on the house or from the local weather service. Where there are no electric blinds yet, the motor needs a specialist firm.

Cost

This service is part of the Konihaus packages — from CHF 39 per month, with installation, setup and ongoing support included. How much your home needs we work out in the free consultation. You then get a quote that lists every item separately. See packages & prices
