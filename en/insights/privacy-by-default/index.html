Privacy by Default

August 15, 2026

Your Data Stays at Home

When you use Google Home or Amazon Alexa, your voice commands, your routines, your preferences—they all flow to the cloud. You're trading convenience for surveillance, whether you realize it or not.

With Konihaus, everything runs on hardware in your home. No cloud. No servers somewhere else processing your patterns. Your smart home data never leaves your living room unless you explicitly tell it to.

How It Works

Your Konihaus system runs on a Home Assistant instance (a lightweight Linux computer, typically a Raspberry Pi or small NUC) installed in your home. All the logic—automations, device coordination, scenes—lives there.

Local-only data flow:

  • Your Zigbee devices talk directly to the local hub
  • Z-Wave devices stay local
  • WiFi devices communicate locally when possible
  • No outbound data collection

The only exception: if you choose to set up remote access (to check your home while away), we use an encrypted VPN tunnel that you control. You're not giving Konihaus access—you're granting yourself secure remote access.

What This Means Practically

No data harvesting. Konihaus has no incentive to collect behavioral data. We don't monetize your attention or sell insights about your living patterns.

No forced cloud updates. Your system doesn't depend on distant servers that might go down or change terms.

No lock-in. You can access your own data directly. It's all open standards (Zigbee, Z-Wave, Matter, WiFi). You own it.

Faster response times. Local processing is instant. No cloud latency for turning on a light or adjusting the thermostat.

Remote Access: Secure and Optional

Want to check on your home while traveling? Konihaus supports a private VPN connection—encrypted end-to-end, using protocols like WireGuard or OpenVPN. You set it up, you control the credentials, you decide when to use it.

This is fundamentally different from cloud-first systems: with Konihaus, you decide if and when to enable remote access. It's not the default mode of operation.

Why We Built It This Way

Privacy isn't a feature we added. It's the foundation. A truly personal smart home shouldn't require trusting a corporation with intimate details of your daily life—what time you wake, when you leave, whether you're home.

With local-first architecture, that trust barrier simply doesn't exist.
